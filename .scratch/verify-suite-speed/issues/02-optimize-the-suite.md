**The headline is that `networkidle` beats the fixed sleeps.** `waitForLoadState("networkidle")`
waits for 500ms of network silence and is discouraged by Playwright's own docs; 141 of them at
~540ms average is where this suite's time actually goes. Start there, not at the sleeps.

**Second headline: 42 visits to `/choose-any-deck`.** That's a per-test setup cost paid over and
over. Whether it can be paid once is a real design question about `workers: 1` and state
isolation — not a free win.

Everything below was known before 01 and is still true.

## Why it matters

`apps/shuffler/verify.sh` takes 3.6–9.5 minutes. That's slow enough that nobody runs it, so
nobody learns whether a change broke something — and red stops meaning anything. Three
table-mode specs were broken for weeks before anyone looked (fixed 2026-08-07; the cause was a
`<details>` disclosure the specs never learned about). That is the actual cost being paid.

## What is already known, so it isn't re-derived

- **It isn't the server.** ~1–2% of wall clock. Measured, not assumed — see ticket 01.
- **The two real server-side costs, small but the only ones:** `tls.connect` (4 spans, **4.5s
  p95**) means the suite still reaches the internet, almost certainly Scryfall; and
  `GET /proxy-image` (4 calls, **2.4s total**) is by far the slowest route per call.
  Everything else is single-digit ms. Cutting the suite's internet dependency is probably the
  one optimization worth doing that doesn't need 01's data first.
- **44 fixed `page.waitForTimeout` sleeps, ~39 seconds of floor**, concentrated in the
  query-parameter-modals (16), library-grouping (14), and mulligan (6) specs. Two of the
  mulligan ones wait 1800ms on a shuffle animation. These are the obvious target — but
  replacing a sleep with a real wait condition is a correctness change per site, not a sweep.
- **The suite has no single duration.** Four consecutive runs, same tests, no changes: 9.5 →
  5.4 → 5.2 → **3.6 minutes**. `data.db` is never reset between runs (nothing sets
  `SQLITE_DB_PATH`, nothing deletes it). So "how slow is it" needs a stated condition — cold or
  warm — before it has an answer, and any optimization number without that condition attached
  is meaningless. Ticket 01 stamps it on the root span for exactly this reason.
- **`workers: 1` and `fullyParallel: false` are deliberate** — "we're testing concurrent
  state." Parallelism is a decision to make with Jess, not a free win to take.
