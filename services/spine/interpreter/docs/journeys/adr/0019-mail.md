# ADR-0019 — Mail: journeys talk to each other through a courier

**Status:** accepted

## Context

Needs ask the world; enactments tell it. The third side of the triangle is
journeys in flight talking to *each other* — a parser feeding a writer, peers
conversing in a detachment. Without a first-class channel, that becomes shared
mutable state, ad-hoc callbacks, or a systemwide bus anybody can overhear — all
shapes this framework exists to end.

## Decision

**Mail moves between journeys under three disciplines: addressed, matched, freight.**

- **Addressed.** Mail goes to a `Journey::Handle` somebody was handed — never onto a
  bus. A handle is opaque (delivery schemes differ per outfit) and grants exactly one
  right: **to post**. It never grants reading another journey's mail. A piece of mail
  is **the payload itself, no envelope** — no automatic `from:`; a sender wanting a
  reply puts a handle *in* the message, because a transport stamping sender handles
  would leak a capability nobody granted. Handles are flight-only.
- **Matched.** `receive(matching:)` takes any `===`-responder (class, Regexp, lambda,
  Archetype); omitted, it matches any mail. "Either of these" is Ruby's own
  `->(mail) { mail in Apple | Orange }` — no combinator needed.
- **Freight.** Every piece of mail must satisfy `Ractor.shareable?` **at post, under
  every outfit** — inline, fiber, spec — enforced at the framework chokepoint before
  any courier sees it (`UnpostableMail`, a `StageError`). No auto-copy: a transparent
  copy would convert an aliasing bug into a heisen-difference between outfits, the
  one divergence the lockstep promise forbids. Mail is a value; the gate is also the
  content rule's enforcement arm — live records and IOs cannot ride, ids can.

**The verbs.** `post(mail, to: handle)` returns `nil` always (fire-and-forget; there
is no `post!` because the courier *is* the operation), never blocks, never raises on
delivery grounds — a sender holds a handle, not a promise. `receive` comes inline
(`@order = receive(matching: OrderMail)`, with `:@ivar` sugar) and declared
(`docket matching: ReplyMail` + `stage receive def apply_reply!(reply)`), the raw
form unit-testable off-manifest.

**The rules that make it safe:**

- **Claims are transactional with the stage** ([ADR-0004](0004-a-boundary-is-a-consistency-point.md)):
  mail claimed by a stage is provisional, committed on commit, returned to the queue
  on every other outcome. So `post` is at-least-once under retry; `receive` is
  exactly-once per commit — you can un-take what nobody has seen, you cannot un-give.
- **An unfulfilled receive parks** — `wait on: Wake.mail(matching:)`
  ([ADR-0020](0020-waits.md)); the halt is a **throw**, not a raise: the raise
  family is fully allocated, and a raise would put "I am waiting" within reach of
  `fail_on` — terminality inferred from a machinery signal. Retry is stage
  re-entrancy; under a suspending conveyance the park becomes a yield with the stage
  body unchanged.
- **Deputization follows the excursion chain only**: an excursed child claims its
  blocked ancestors' mail (own queue first, then nearest ancestor; arrival order
  within a queue), so processing can move into a sub-journey with no sender
  noticing. A **detached child never deputizes** — N deputies racing for one letter
  is framework-introduced nondeterminism.
- **A dead journey's mail is held and announced, never rerouted.** Parent pickup
  would re-route on topology the sender never saw and decide flow by death rather
  than declaration. `:mail_unclaimed` notifies (handle and mail class, never
  content); diagnostics make it visible.
- **No framework shutdown mail.** A default-handled mail that ends a run is inferred
  terminality plus ambient flow control. Out-of-band ending is `abandon!`; in-band
  wind-down is the journey's own declared mail class, matched and acted on in its
  own loop.
- **The courier is an outfit scope** — one per tree, shared by reference; replacing
  it at a derivation draws a postal boundary. The null kit carries a real in-memory
  courier (a null courier would make `post` a silent drop — the one behavior no
  courier is allowed). Every rule above lives at framework chokepoints, not in the
  courier, so a lazy courier cannot repeal one.

**Testing is the proof of fitness**: specs post real mail through the real courier —
nothing substituted, so a green spec is evidence about matching, ordering, claims,
and freight. The **step cap** (a strict test-mode diagnostic listener, default 100
attempts) turns an accidental infinite mail loop into `StepCapExceeded` carrying the
log's tail — the loop's own confession — instead of a hung CI job. Durable mail,
receive timeouts (compose a deadline wake instead), priorities, and supervision are
deliberately out of scope.

## Consequences

- Smuggling live objects between journeys, reading someone else's inbox, ambient
  broadcast, mail-driven terminality, and losing mail to a snag are impossible by
  construction.
- Fan-out is built *on top*, as a journey that receives and re-posts — forwarding
  stated in the open.
- Entry points outside a journey spell `outfit.courier.post` — no facade sugar.
